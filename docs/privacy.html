MoneyPaws Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

MoneyPaws ("the app") is a rewards app. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. Contact: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com

Information we collect

Account data: your email address and password (stored securely by our provider, Supabase).

Activity data: your reward balance, login streak, game plays, steps count you choose to claim, referral codes, and withdrawal requests.

Payout details: the UNO Wallet ID or email you enter when you request a withdrawal.

Advertising data: the app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

How we use it

To run your account and rewards, prevent fraud and abuse, process withdrawals, show ads, and improve the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for hosting and Google AdMob for ads), and when required by law. Withdrawals are sent to the UNO Wallet you provide; UNO is a third-party service with its own privacy policy.

Step counting

Step counts are estimated from your phone's motion sensor while the app is open and stored on your device until you claim them.

Your choices and deleting your account

You can delete your account and data at any time in the app: Profile → Delete my account. You can also email us at the address above. We keep withdrawal records without your account link for bookkeeping. Ad personalization can be changed in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

The app is not for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Rewards disclaimer

Rewards are small and limited per day, withdrawals have a minimum amount, and payouts are reviewed manually and may be delayed or refused for suspected fraud.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
