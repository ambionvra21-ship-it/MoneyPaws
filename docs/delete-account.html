Delete your MoneyPaws account

You can delete your MoneyPaws account and its data at any time.

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

Open MoneyPaws, go to Profile, and tap Delete my account. Confirm the message. Your account is removed right away. If you have a pending cash-out, wait until it is processed first.

Option 2: by email

Send an email from the address you registered with to moneypaws.app.help@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your profile, email address, PawCoins balance, login streak, referral code and links, and ad and game counters.

What is kept

Records of cash-out requests that were already paid are kept without any link to your account, for bookkeeping. Remaining PawCoins have no cash value after deletion.

See also our Privacy Policy.
