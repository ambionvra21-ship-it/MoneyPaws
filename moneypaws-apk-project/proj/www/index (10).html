Terms & Privacy

Rewards. Rewards are small, limited per day, and paid from ad revenue. They are not a salary or guaranteed income. Cash-out amounts run from ₱5 to ₱1,000, one per day. Each amount needs enough PawCoins plus a minimum number of rewarded ads watched, and larger amounts also need an older account (see Cash Out for the full list). Payouts go to the UNO Wallet, GCash or Maya account you provide, are reviewed manually, usually within 24 to 48 hours and up to 72 hours, and may be delayed. Each wallet number can be linked to one MoneyPaws account only.

Fair use. One account per person. Fake accounts, self-referrals, bots, emulators, or any ad-click manipulation lead to a ban and loss of balance. Referral bonuses (₱0.50 for you and your friend) unlock after the friend watches 10 rewarded ads.

Data we collect. Email, reward activity, referral codes, withdrawal details (including a QR image if you upload one), and ad data collected by Google AdMob (such as your advertising ID). We do not sell personal data. Hosting is by Supabase. UNO is a third-party wallet with its own policy.

Steps. Estimated from your phone's motion sensor while the app is open.

Your choices. Delete your account any time in Profile → Delete my account. Not for users under 18. Contact: moneypaws.app.help@gmail.com

Help & Community

📘 Join our Facebook page & see payout proofs

Share MoneyPaws with friends:

How do I earn? Mine every 3 hours, watch rewarded ads, claim your daily login, spin, scratch cards, Fruit Tap, steps, the daily mission, and referrals.

Why are there ads? Ads pay for the rewards. More ads watched means more PawCoins for you.

How do I cash out? Open Cash Out and pick an amount and a wallet (UNO, GCash or Maya). Every amount needs enough PawCoins (100 PawCoins = ₱1) and a minimum number of rewarded ads watched. Larger amounts also need an older account. The list on that page shows what is left to unlock.

Why is my cash-out pending? We review each request by hand and send it to your wallet, usually within 24 to 48 hours and up to 72 hours. Payout proofs are posted on our Facebook page.

Delete my account? Profile, then Delete my account.

Need help? Email moneypaws.app.help@gmail.com or message our Facebook page.
